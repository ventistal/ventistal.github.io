Registration Form

Workflows

Workflows and processes associated with registration.

Main workflow

  1. Create registration request — a Ventistål employee creates a request with the stakeholder's email and registration type (Customer or Supplier).
  2. Send invitation — the stakeholder receives an email with a secure, single-use link to the registration form.
  3. Stakeholder completes the form — the stakeholder enters and reviews the required information.
  4. Submit registration — the stakeholder confirms and submits; the registration becomes read-only and Ventistål is notified.

Ventistål processing

Supplier

Submitted → Reviewed → Registered in Ventistål systems

If accepted, the supplier information is entered/integrated into the relevant internal systems.

Customer

Submitted │ ▼ Customer validation │ ├── Invalid → Rejected │ └── Valid │ ▼ Registered in internal systems

Customers require an additional validation step before they can be registered internally.

Correction workflow

Submitted │ ▼ Ventistål review │ ▼ Correction required │ ▼ Stakeholder receives new link │ ▼ Updates information │ ▼ Resubmits
