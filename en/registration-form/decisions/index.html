Registration Form

Decisions

Design and product decisions made during development, with their rationale.

Decided

  • Responsibility for providing accurate information moves from Ventistål employees to the customer or supplier themselves; Ventistål retains control over validation and final registration.
  • The application supports exactly two registration types — Supplier and Customer — with the process differing between them after submission.
  • Stakeholders access the form through a secure, single-use link rather than a login/account.
  • Customer registrations require an explicit validation step before they can be registered internally; supplier registrations go straight from review to registration.
  • Corrections are handled by sending the stakeholder a new secure link rather than having a Ventistål employee edit the submitted data manually.
  • For the MVP, no status should be added unless it corresponds to a real business state (see the status list).
  • BankID, e-signatures, integrations and specific authentication technology are explicitly not being specified yet — they are deferred beyond this story.

Pending business decisions

Before this story can become a detailed specification, Ventistål needs to clarify:

  1. What exact data is required for suppliers?
  2. What exact data is required for customers?
  3. What makes a customer "valid"?
  4. Who performs the customer validation?
  5. What happens after validation?
  6. Which internal systems must receive the data?
  7. Is integration required, or is manual registration acceptable for the MVP?
  8. What happens when information is incorrect?
  9. Can stakeholders save an incomplete form?
  10. How long are invitation links valid?
  11. How is the stakeholder's identity/trust established?
  12. How should banking information be handled?
  13. What notifications/emails are required?
  14. How are duplicate customers/suppliers handled?
  15. What information must Ventistål retain for audit purposes?

Risks still to resolve

  • Identity of the stakeholder — how much trust does Ventistål place in mere possession of the email link, especially for sensitive data such as banking details?
  • Duplicate registrations — which identifier (e.g. organisation number) should be used to detect an existing registration for the same company?
  • Expired links — the system should let a stakeholder request a new invitation without an employee having to recreate the whole request.
  • Wrong email address — a recipient should not be able to transfer a sensitive registration to another person without an appropriate mechanism.
  • Abandoned registrations — Ventistål should be able to identify requests that were opened but never submitted, and decide whether reminders are sent.
