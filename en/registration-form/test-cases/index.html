Traceability / audit

  • TC-18 — Registration history: the system must let Ventistål determine who created the request, when, which email it was sent to, when it was opened, when information was submitted, who reviewed it, what decision was made, and when it was registered/rejected/returned.
  • TC-19 — Submitted data: Ventistål must be able to identify exactly which information was submitted by the stakeholder.
  • TC-20 — Correction history: if information is corrected, Ventistål must be able to distinguish the latest information from previous submissions where required for traceability.
