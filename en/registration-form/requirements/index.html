Registration Form

Requirements

Functional and non-functional requirements for the registration form.

Registration types

The application must support two registration types, each with its own downstream process: Supplier registration and Customer registration.

Process requirements

  1. Ventistål must be able to initiate a registration request by providing the stakeholder's email address and the registration type (Customer or Supplier).
  2. The system must send the stakeholder an invitation email containing a secure link to the registration form.
  3. The stakeholder must be able to enter the required information through that link.
  4. The stakeholder must be able to review the information before submitting it.
  5. The submitted information must be sent back to Ventistål for processing according to its registration type.
  6. The process must provide sufficient status and traceability at every step.

Data requirements

Supplier

  • Company information
  • Contact information
  • Registration/VAT information
  • Banking/payment information
  • Required declarations/documents

Customer

  • Company information
  • Contact information
  • Registration/VAT information
  • Other information required to determine whether the customer is valid

Non-functional requirements

  • The invitation link must be unique to the registration request and usable only once, or become unusable after successful submission.
  • The link must expire after a defined period.
  • The link must not expose sensitive information through the URL.
  • Once submitted, the registration must become read-only for the stakeholder.
  • A confirmation must be shown to the stakeholder after submission.

Open questions

  • Should the employee also provide the company name, organisation number, contact person or other initial information when creating the request?
  • How long should the invitation link remain valid, and what happens when it expires?
  • Can Ventistål resend the invitation, and does resending invalidate the previous link?
  • Can the stakeholder save the form and continue later?
  • What happens if the email address was entered incorrectly?
  • Should the stakeholder receive a confirmation email after submission?
