Customer

User stories related to the customer in the registration process.

Main story

As a Ventistål employee, I want to request customer information from an external stakeholder through a controlled digital process, so that the stakeholder can provide their own information and Ventistål can subsequently validate and register it in the appropriate internal systems.

Customer stories

  • As a customer, I want to receive a secure, single-use link so that I can submit my company's information without going through a Ventistål employee.
  • As a customer, I want to review my information before submitting it, so that I can correct any mistakes.
  • As a customer, I want confirmation once I have submitted the registration, so that I know Ventistål has received it.
  • As a customer whose registration requires correction, I want to receive a new secure link, so that I can update only the information Ventistål has flagged.

Information collected

  • Company information
  • Contact information
  • Registration/VAT information
  • Other information required to determine whether the customer is valid

Customer validation workflow

Submitted │ ▼ Customer validation │ ├── Invalid → Rejected │ └── Valid │ ▼ Registered in internal systems

Open question

What exactly constitutes a "valid customer"? This needs to be defined as a business rule rather than assumed.
