Supplier

User stories related to the supplier in the registration process.

Main story

As a Ventistål employee, I want to request supplier information from an external stakeholder through a controlled digital process, so that the stakeholder can provide their own information and Ventistål can subsequently validate and register it in the appropriate internal systems.

Supplier stories

  • As a supplier, I want to receive a secure, single-use link so that I can submit my company, contact, VAT and banking information without going through a Ventistål employee.
  • As a supplier, I want to review my information before submitting it, so that I can correct any mistakes.
  • As a supplier, I want confirmation once I have submitted the registration, so that I know Ventistål has received it.
  • As a supplier whose registration requires correction, I want to receive a new secure link, so that I can update only the information Ventistål has flagged.

Information collected

  • Company information
  • Contact information
  • Registration/VAT information
  • Banking/payment information
  • Required declarations/documents

Supplier processing workflow

Submitted → Reviewed → Registered in Ventistål systems

Open question
